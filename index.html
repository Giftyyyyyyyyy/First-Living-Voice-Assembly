Who We Are

Speaking Life. Building Faith. Transforming Destinies.

First Living Voice Assembly is a place where the Word of God comes alive, faith is strengthened and people discover their purpose in Christ.

We believe in the transforming power of God's Word, the importance of fellowship and the call to live a life that reflects His glory.
